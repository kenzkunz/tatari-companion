# Sparkle duplicate reward correction

14 October 2026: user-provided game-result screenshot explicitly says an already-owned Sparkle skin gives 5 Glitter Fruits. The screenshot has a New banner as well, but the duplicate compensation sentence is explicit. Its game version and the general reward rule are not established.

Removed the guide's universal one-fruit duplicate claim. HotSpringParam parameter 5 is labelled RerollCost in spa-audit.json and holds an asset quantity of 1; it does not establish duplicate compensation. UnitAurora includes skin rarity, but no duplicate reward field. No evidence currently confirms that 5 is universal or specifically the rare-skin payout. Do not derive that from unlabelled UnitParam values.
